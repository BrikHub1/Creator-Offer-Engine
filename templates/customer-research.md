## Core questions to ask
1. What's the biggest challenge you're facing with [problem area] right now?
2. What have you already tried to solve it?
3. What didn't work, and why?
4. If you had a magic wand, what would the ideal solution look like?
5. Have you paid for a solution before? What did you pay, and was it worth it?
6. What would stop you from buying a solution like this?
7. Where do you currently go for advice or information on this topic?
