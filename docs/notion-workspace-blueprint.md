# Notion Workspace Blueprint

The Creator Offer Engine workspace is a single Notion sidebar with 14 emoji-labeled, interlinked pages. Each page is self-contained but feeds the next — the output of one step becomes the input of the next.

---

## Page 1 — Start Here: 10-Minute Offer Sprint

**Purpose:** Get the user a tangible first result in under 10 minutes — a named offer with a price and a one-sentence pitch.

**Inputs:** Skill, who they help, biggest outcome they can deliver.
**Outputs:** Offer name, target-customer sentence, price range, value prop, shareable summary card.

**Template fields:**
- What's the one skill you're most confident in? (SKILL)
- Who specifically do you help? (AUDIENCE)
- What result can you reliably help them get? (OUTCOME)
- How long does it take? (TIMEFRAME)
- What's your gut price? (PRICE)

---

## Page 2 — Niche & Customer Research Dashboard

**Purpose:** Validate a niche using real-world evidence instead of guesswork.

**Structure:**
- Toggle: My Niche Options (3 options)
- Database: Competitor Tracker (Name, Platform, Offer, Price, Differentiator, Weakness)
- Database: Customer Language Bank (Phrase, Source, Emotion, Category)
- Summary: Validated Niche Statement

---

## Page 3 — Offer Builder

**Purpose:** Turn a vague idea into a specific, deliverable, priced offer.

**Fields:** Offer name, who it's for, deliverables (max 5), format (digital download / done-for-you / coaching call / subscription), price, what's NOT included, one-line pitch (AI-generated).

---

## Page 4 — Product Naming Generator

**Purpose:** Generate 10 name options and pick the best.

**Frameworks:** Outcome Method, Audience-Verb-Audience-Gets-Paid, Number-Noun, Emotion-trigger name, Your-Name System.

---

## Page 5 — Pricing Calculator

**Purpose:** Find a confident price using value-based and market-anchored logic.

**Frameworks:**
1. Time-based: hourly rate × hours × 1.5 for digital delivery
2. Value-based: client outcome value ÷ 10 (rule of thumb)
3. Market-anchored: competitor mid-point × 0.8, grow from there
4. Beginner rule: price at "uncomfortable but not laughable"

---

## Page 6 — Landing Page Copy Builder

**Purpose:** Build a complete 7-section landing page with fill-in-the-blank templates and prompts.

**Sections:** Hero Headline, Subheadline, Problem, What You Get, How It Works, Who It's For / Not For, Pricing CTA, FAQ, Testimonial block (placeholder until real proof exists).

---

## Page 7 — Content Calendar

**Purpose:** Generate 30 days of platform-specific content ideas tied to the offer.

**Structure:** Day, Platform, Format, Hook, Body summary, CTA, Status.
**Content types:** Pain-point post, tutorial, before/after, social proof, myth-bust, behind-the-scenes.

---

## Page 8 — Short-Form Video Hook Bank

**Purpose:** A swipe file of 50 hooks organized by goal: attract, educate, convert.

**Categories:** Attract, Pain, Curiosity, Proof, Offer (use sparingly).

---

## Page 9 — Lead Magnet Builder

**Purpose:** Create a free resource that attracts email subscribers and warms them toward purchase.

**Structure:** Lead magnet type (checklist / swipe file / template / mini-guide / quiz), title generator, 5-section outline, delivery instructions.

---

## Page 10 — Email Sequence Builder

**Purpose:** Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers.

**Structure:** Day 0 deliver + set expectations, Day 1 story, Day 3 teach + soft pitch, Day 5 objection + proof, Day 7 direct offer + CTA.

---

## Page 11 — Sales Tracker

**Purpose:** Track every lead, conversation, and sale in one place.

**Database:** Name, Platform, Date contacted, Status (Lead / Interested / Proposal sent / Sold / Lost), Revenue, Notes.
**Views:** Kanban by status, table by date, summary (total revenue, conversion rate).

---

## Page 12 — Testimonial Collector

**Purpose:** Capture and organize real customer feedback for marketing use.

**Structure:** Name, Date, Platform, Result, Quote, Permission to use?, Used in?
**Template:** "After using [PRODUCT], [NAME] was able to [RESULT] in [TIMEFRAME]."

---

## Page 13 — Weekly Review Dashboard

**Purpose:** A 15-minute weekly review to track what's working and adjust.

**Structure:** Week, Revenue, Posts, Leads, Conversions, Top Win, Biggest Problem, Next Focus.

---

## Page 14 — Resource Vault

**Purpose:** Curated free tools and resources by category: build your offer, write with AI, design, email marketing, analytics, communities.
