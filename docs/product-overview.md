| | |
|---|---|

## Ideal Customer Profile

**Who:** Early-20s to mid-30s, US/English-speaking, active on TikTok/YouTube creator-economy content. Has a marketable skill (web design, video editing, social media, writing, photography) and an account, but no product and no consistent income from it.

**Demographics**
- 18–34, US-based, English-speaking
- Student, side-hustler, or newly self-employed
- Earns $0–$2,000/month online, or wants to start
- Already has Canva, ChatGPT, and Notion

**Pain points**
- "I don't know what to sell or how to package it"
- "I've watched 50 YouTube videos and still haven't started"
- "My offer feels generic — I don't know how to stand out"
- "I'm scared of selling and don't know how to pitch myself"

**Desires**
- A clear niche they feel confident in
- An offer they can describe in one sentence
- A landing page that doesn't embarrass them
- Their first sale, even if it's $19

**Objections**
- "I don't have enough experience yet"
- "The market is too saturated"
- "AI prompts don't work for me — the output is always generic"

**Buying triggers**
- Seeing a creator post their first sale screenshot
- Feeling stuck after 3 months of "preparing"
- A free tool that gives them a taste of a real result

## Positioning

> Generic prompts give you advice. Creator Offer Engine gives you a system.

Every prompt is pre-engineered with the exact context, constraints, and output format a beginner creator needs. The Notion workspace chains these prompts into a workflow — the output of your niche sprint feeds your offer builder, which feeds your landing-page copy, which feeds your content calendar. Nothing is disconnected.

**Before → After**

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| A skill but no idea what to sell | A specific, named offer with a price and audience |
| A blank "Business Ideas" Notion page | A 7-section landing page ready to publish |
| Posting random content hoping something sticks | A 30-day content calendar tied to a specific offer |
| Scared to pitch or DM anyone | A ready-to-send DM script and email sequence |

**Positioning statement:** If you have a skill but no offer, no landing page, and no idea what to post — Creator Offer Engine is the one workspace that connects all of that in a single weekend. No course. No coach. No guessing.

**Tier 1 — Free: Offer Clarity Starter**
- Offer Sprint worksheet (PDF + Notion page)

- Basic niche selector framework
- One value-prop formula
- CTA to upgrade to the full system

**Tier 2 — $29: Creator Offer Engine**
- Full Notion workspace (14 sections)
- 30-prompt AI library
- 7-day action plan
- Landing-page copy builder
- 30-day content calendar
- 5-email sequence templates
- Swipe file (headlines, CTAs, DMs, hooks)
- PDF quick-start guide
- Lifetime minor updates

**Tier 3 — $69: Creator Offer Engine Pro**
- Everything in Tier 2
- Commercial rights (resell templates to clients)
- 3 extra niche-specific Notion templates
- 15 bonus prompts (client work, retainer pitching, testimonials)
- Canva landing-page mockup kit
- 3 done-for-you offer example walkthroughs
- Priority access to future templates
- Affiliate program access (30% commission)
