# Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need Notion Premium?**
No. The free tier of Notion works for everything in this workspace.

**Do I need to know how to use AI/ChatGPT?**
No experience needed. Every prompt includes instructions and an example. Copy, paste, and edit — that's it.

**What if I don't have a social media following?**
The system works from zero followers. Several content formats are designed specifically for people starting from scratch.

**What kind of offer will I build?**
That depends on your skill. Past users have built UGC scriptwriting services, Notion template packs, social media management retainers, photography packages, and lead generation services. The system adapts to your skill.

**Can I use the templates with my own clients?**
Pro tier only. The Pro tier includes commercial rights so you can white-label the templates for client projects.

**What if I get stuck?**
The troubleshooting section in the workspace covers the most common points of confusion. If you're still stuck, use the contact email to reach out.

**Is there ongoing coaching or community?**
Not at this price point. This is a self-guided system. A community option may be added in the future.

**What's your refund policy?**
30 days, no questions asked, as long as you've genuinely tried the system.
