# AI Prompt Library (30 Prompts)

All prompts use `[BRACKETS]` for variables. Replace with your specifics before running each one in ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity.

---

## Category A — Niche Selection (Prompts 1–5)

**1. The Niche Clarity Sprint** — *Use when you have 3 ideas and can't pick one.*
Evaluates niche options on market demand, competitive advantage, content potential, and monetization clarity. Scores each 1–10 and recommends one with reasoning.

**2. Sub-Niche Drill-Down** — *Use when your niche is too broad.*
Generates 8 specific sub-niches with audience, core problem, offer name idea, and a hook for each.

**3. Niche Validation Check** — *Use to validate before committing.*
Generates likely audience questions, current (flawed) solutions, existing competitors, red flags, and green flags — then a validation score out of 10.

**4. Competitor Gap Finder** — *Use to find a differentiator in a crowded niche.*
Analyzes 3 competitors for strengths, gaps, and underserved audiences, then generates 2–3 positioning gaps you could own.

**5. Validated Niche Statement Generator** — *Use after research is done.*
Produces a validated niche statement, a 15-word elevator pitch, and a niche clarity summary for your profile.

## Category B — Customer Research (Prompts 6–8)

**6. Customer Voice Miner** — Generates the exact phrases, fears, failed attempts, objections, and post-purchase language of your ideal customer, in their own words.

**7. Ideal Customer Day-in-the-Life** — Writes a 300–400 word narrative of your ideal customer's day, ending with them buying your product.

**8. Offer-Market Fit Checker** — Pressure-tests your offer concept on clarity, specificity, value gap, competition, and demand signal, then rewrites a stronger version.

---

## Category C — Offer Creation (Prompts 9–12)

**9. The Offer Builder** — Builds a complete offer: name options, one-line pitch, deliverables, scope boundaries, ICP, exclusions, and a "how it works" section.

**10. Offer Stack Builder** — Designs a 3-tier offer stack (free / core / premium) with upgrade hooks between tiers.

**11. Productized Service Designer** — Turns a custom freelance service into a repeatable, scoped, timeline-driven productized offer.

**12. The Anti-Generic Offer Differentiator** — Rewrites a generic-sounding offer using a unique mechanism, a specific timeframed result, a proof hook, and an exclusion that creates contrast.
