# Creator Offer Engine

**Turn one skill into a clear, sellable offer in one weekend.**

Creator Offer Engine is an AI-powered Notion workspace and prompt-library system that helps aspiring creators, freelancers, and digital beginners go from "I have a skill but nothing to sell" to a named offer, priced product, landing-page copy, and 30-day content plan — without a course, a coach, or months of guessing.

> Your first offer shouldn't take six months.

---

## The Problem

Most aspiring creators aren't stuck because they lack talent — they're stuck because they don't have a system. Generic AI prompts produce vague, disconnected advice. Courses are long and get abandoned. Nothing chains research → offer → copy → content into one workflow.

## The Solution

Creator Offer Engine replaces disconnected advice with a **connected system**: a 14-page Notion workspace, a 30-prompt AI library, and ready-to-use templates that chain together — the output of one step becomes the input of the next.

## Who It's For

- Aspiring creators and freelancers with a skill (design, writing, video editing, social media, photography) but no packaged offer
